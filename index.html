04. About Me

I'm a UI/UX designer who specializes in designing apps. I care about the small moments: the empty state, the error message, the tap that feels just right.

I work closely with product managers and engineers to ship experiences that are both beautiful and practical. When I'm not designing, I'm studying great products and figuring out why they work.
